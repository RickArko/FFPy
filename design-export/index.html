FFPy Pick'em · Results panel redesign

Three readings of the same data.

All three variants share theme.css: same palette, same type stack, same scale. They differ in emphasis — what the eye lands on first, how dense the ledger feels, how loudly outcomes shout.

→ Open the side-by-side compare view for iteration. Current preferred direction: Variant C · Stadium.

A · Almanac

Editorial restraint

Engraved metric cards, gold rule lines, old-style numerals in the ledger. Most "vintage almanac." Quiet authority.

 B · Broadsheet

Headline + deck

Newspaper masthead. Win rate as a sub-headline figure beside an italic deck. Densest ledger. Most prose-forward.

 C · Stadium

Scoreboard energy

Deep teal hero with a monospaced win-rate. Per-week bars, scoreboard-card leaderboard. Most live-game.
